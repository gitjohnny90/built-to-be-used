Why single files instead of a course platform

Because platforms are feeds wearing graduation caps. They want you logged in, notified, and enrolled in the next thing. A file wants nothing from you. You own it like you'd own a workbook: print it, keep it, finish it in a week or come back to it in a year. The format is the philosophy.
