How tradesmen actually use AI (without the hype)

Over 70% of contractors have tested AI, and the #1 barrier — named by 44% — is lack of training (ServiceTitan, 2026 "AI in the Trades" report). The gap isn't the tools; it's knowing what to ask them.

Three places AI pays off fastest in a trade business

  • Scopes of work. Nearly every payment dispute starts with work the customer assumed was included. AI writes the "included / excluded / customer responsibilities" list in two minutes, every job.
  • The follow-up text. Most ignored quotes aren't rejections — they're inertia. A well-written, non-pushy follow-up sequence wins real jobs.
  • Review replies. Replying to every review, good and bad, is one of the strongest local-SEO signals you control — and the easiest to automate well.
  • The hard rule: never trust AI on code compliance, electrical, gas, or structural questions. Verify with your local building department. Good prompts have that boundary built in.
