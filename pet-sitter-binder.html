What your pet sitter actually needs written down

"Text me if anything seems off" works right up until your phone dies in a canyon, the wifi drops, or the sitter doesn't know that your dog hides under the bed during storms and that's normal.

What to leave a sitter (that everyone forgets)

  • Exact feeding amounts, not "a scoop." Whose scoop? Write cups or grams, and where the food lives.
  • A signed vet consent note. Many clinics hesitate to treat without owner authorization. A simple note with your card-on-file instructions removes the worst-case bottleneck.
  • Poison-control numbers. ASPCA (888-426-4435) and Pet Poison Helpline (855-764-7661) — both staffed 24/7, both worth taping to the fridge.
  • The quirks list. What's normal-weird for your pet versus emergency-weird. Sitters can't tell the difference without you.
  • House basics. Wifi password, trash day, thermostat. Every text the sitter doesn't have to send is a better trip for both of you.
