Most AI-at-work advice is written for solo operators. This course is for the person responsible for other people — while AI changes what their people's work looks like, and while every silence from leadership gets filled by rumors and 2 a.m. fears.

What two weeks with your real crew changes

  • You map your people before you map the tools. Each person's working geniuses, competencies, and frustrations — because delegation to AI should follow human strengths, not org charts.
  • You learn the pairing move. Send each person's draining work to the machine and reinvest the hours in what they're genuinely good at.
  • You face the apprenticeship problem. If AI does all the junior work, how do juniors become masters? Almost nobody else is teaching this, and it decides what your crew looks like in five years.
  • You answer credit and blame before the first incident — who owns human-plus-machine work, and what happens when the tool gets it wrong.
  • You end with a signed, one-page Crew Code — working agreements and AI rules your team helped write.
